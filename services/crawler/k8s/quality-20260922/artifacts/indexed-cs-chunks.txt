 ### Satisfactory Progress Towards Degree  Upon having attempted 72 semester hours (including transfer, advanced placement, advanced standing, credit by examination, course withdrawal), "satisfactory progress" toward this degree will include passing the following:  - Be enrolled in a degree granting-program, and  - Be certified at the close of the academic year by their department as making satisfactory progress toward a degree based upon preestablished minimum criteria, and  - Have an in-major GPA of 2.00 or above and maintain an in-major GPA of 2.00 or above until graduation.  ### Graduation Requirements  To qualify for a B.S. degree in CS, a student must:  - Complete 123 credit hours  - Earn a “C” (2.0) or better in CS 1114 Introduction to Software Design , CS 2104 Introduction to Problem Solving in Computer Science , CS 2114 Software Design and Data Structures , CS 2505 Introduction
